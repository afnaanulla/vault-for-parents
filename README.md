# 🏦 SecureVault — Private Financial Vault for Parents

A simple, beautiful, and secure offline vault app designed with love for parents to store all their banking details, ATM PINs, card numbers, and important government IDs without stress or confusion.

Inspired by the clean, minimalist design of modern banking apps like Jupiter, **SecureVault** is made specifically to be **elderly-friendly, hassle-free, and 100% private**.

---

## 💖 Why Parents Love SecureVault

Before SecureVault, remembering dozens of account numbers, 4-digit ATM PINs, mobile banking MPINs, and customer IDs meant writing them down on scrap papers, in fragile physical diaries, or searching through unorganized WhatsApp chats.

With SecureVault, parents have **one safe, trusted home** for all their important records:

* 👴 **Effortless for Elders:** Large buttons, high-contrast readable text, and simple single-tap actions — no confusing menus or tech jargon.
* 👆 **One-Touch Fingerprint Unlock:** No need to type complicated passwords. A simple touch of their finger unlocks their details in under a second.
* 🔒 **Complete Privacy (Father & Mother Isolation):** Father's bank details and Mother's bank details are kept 100% private from each other. Neither parent can see or accidentally edit the other's records.
* 📴 **100% Offline & Safe:** SecureVault never connects to the internet or uploads data to any cloud. Everything stays securely on the phone itself.
* 🛡️ **No Shoulder-Surfing:** Sensitive PINs, CVVs, and passwords stay masked (`••••`). When tapped, they only reveal after a fingerprint scan and automatically hide themselves after 30 seconds.

---

## 📱 How Parents Use It Every Day

Using SecureVault is as natural and easy as unlocking a phone:

### 1. Pick Your Profile
On the welcome screen, tap **Father 👨** or **Mother 👩**.

### 2. Unlock with Fingerprint or 4-Digit PIN
Rest your finger on the sensor or tap your easy 4-digit PIN on the large numbers pad.

### 3. View Your Details Instantly
All your accounts are neatly arranged into clean categories:
* 🏦 **Bank Accounts:** Account Number, IFSC Code, Branch, and Mobile Banking MPIN.
* 💳 **Debit & Credit Cards:** Card Number, Expiry Date, CVV, and ATM PIN.
* 📱 **UPI & NetBanking:** Google Pay / PhonePe UPI IDs, UPI PIN, and Login User IDs.
* 🪪 **Government IDs:** Aadhaar Numbers and PAN Cards.
* 📸 **Documents & Photos:** Bank passbooks, cancelled cheques, electricity bills, tax receipts, or ID cards. Parents can take a quick photo or upload from gallery, give it a simple name (e.g. *"SBI Passbook Front Page"*), and search or zoom in anytime!
* 📝 **Safe Notes:** Locker combinations, secret codes, and important reminders.

### 4. Fast Single-Tap Copy & Full-Screen Photo Zoom
* Need to share your IFSC code or Account Number? Just tap the **Copy icon 📋** next to the number!
* Need to see tiny print on a cheque or passbook photo? Just tap the photo to open the **Full-Screen Zoom Viewer** with large **[ + ]** and **[ − ]** buttons and 90° rotation!
